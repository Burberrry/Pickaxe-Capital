# Pickaxe Website Reset Blueprint

Status: Docs-only product blueprint.
Date: 2026-06-21.
Authority: CEO B.
Runtime authority: None.

## Current Diagnosis

The application has a working static/manual research engine, strong safety doctrine, and documented route ownership. Its first impression is weaker than its underlying system because the homepage is also the Alerts Desk, internal navigation dominates, terminology is dense, public and internal audiences are mixed, and visual hierarchy competes with module breadth.

The reset must preserve the engine while creating a cleaner front door above it.

## Two-World Architecture

### Public World — Pickaxe Capital

Purpose: explain the company, founder-led operating doctrine, current product value, trust model, proof of work, and one clear next action.

Public language should lead with Pickaxe Capital and describe the internal system in plain terms before naming AI Habitat OS.

### Internal World — AI Habitat OS

Purpose: operate Alerts, Research, Finance Terminal, Source Hub, Archive, Learning Ledger, Staging / QA, and Agent Habitat under source, risk, and CEO B review gates.

The internal world may remain information-dense, but it needs grouped navigation and a clear boundary from the public site.

## Simplified Public Navigation

Recommended public labels for a future implementation:

1. Home
2. What Pickaxe Does
3. Research Process
4. Founder / CEO B
5. Proof of Work
6. Enter Research OS

Do not expose every internal route in the public primary navigation. The exact route mapping requires a separate navigation audit.

## Simplified Internal OS Grouping

- **Decide:** Alerts Desk, Finance Terminal
- **Research:** Research Desk, Watchlists, Signals
- **Verify:** Source Hub, Risk Rules
- **Remember:** Archive, Learning Ledger
- **Operate:** Mission Control, Staging / QA
- **Systems:** Agent Habitat, AI Habitat OS, Vision Map
- **Future / Reference:** placeholders, roadmap, experiments, and local-only prototypes

This grouping is a label model, not route-change authorization.

## Homepage Reset Direction

The future homepage should:

- lead with `Pickaxe Capital`;
- state in one sentence that Pickaxe builds private, source-first research-intelligence systems;
- explain the research loop in three to five steps;
- show concise proof of working static/manual capabilities;
- explain CEO B manual review and no-broker boundary;
- use one primary call to action and one secondary entry to the internal OS;
- keep internal module lists below the first impression.

Recommended first-screen order:

1. brand and concise value proposition;
2. trust/process line;
3. primary action;
4. restrained visual signature;
5. short proof signal.

## Founder / CEO B Usage

- Brand-led homepage by default.
- CEO B may appear as a scarce symbolic command signal, not a repeating mascot.
- Founder B human materials remain private and approval-required.
- The gold mask is invariant; outfit follows context.
- Generated masked art must not be described as documentary photography.

## Official Logo Usage

- Source of truth: circular crossed-pickaxe Pickaxe Capital badge.
- Active public source: `brand/pickaxe-capital-logo.png` and `public/brand/pickaxe-capital-logo.png`.
- Do not invent a substitute mark or promote redundant alternates.
- Resolve alternate/source asset inconsistency in a separate asset-lock sprint if CEO B prioritizes it.

## Visual Design System

- black, charcoal, and gunmetal base;
- metallic, antique, and champagne gold structure;
- silver/white and ivory typography;
- emerald only for verified/approved state;
- crimson only for blocked/risk state;
- fewer simultaneous borders, glows, panels, and labels;
- larger whitespace and clearer type scale;
- restrained transforms and opacity motion;
- one global Starlight canvas with readability protected;
- premium institutional photography/art direction, never generic SaaS or cyberpunk clutter.

## Mobile Reset Rules

- Public first screen must not be delayed by a large internal sidebar.
- Keep one clear heading, short copy, and one primary action above the fold.
- Avoid horizontal navigation dependence and wide dashboard assumptions.
- Maintain at least readable/tappable control sizing under the project QA standard.
- Preserve no-overflow behavior at `390x844`.
- Crop CEO B art safely around the full mask.
- Reduce decorative density before reducing text legibility.

## Remove From The First Impression

- internal completion tracker;
- full route inventory;
- long module lists;
- Staging/build language;
- future-provider or automation concepts;
- repeated operating-system names;
- placeholder routes;
- technical architecture detail;
- speculative public-company language.

## Preserve

- static-first architecture and GitHub Pages deployment;
- canonical hash routes and direct-path bridges;
- root/public mirror contracts;
- source, risk, and CEO B review doctrine;
- V3.1, Phase 9A, Phase 9B, and current LocalStorage contracts;
- official logo and CEO B identity rules;
- one global Starlight field;
- Archive and Learning Ledger lineage;
- clear demo/static/manual labeling.

## Redesign Sequence

1. Lock docs and naming.
2. Verify the docs after an authorized push.
3. Simplify public homepage copy only.
4. Audit public versus internal navigation labels.
5. Define public shell and internal-shell boundary.
6. Clean Founder / CEO B presentation.
7. Simplify the internal OS landing and grouping.
8. Refine one route at a time with desktop/mobile QA.
9. Connect a domain only after the front door is stable.

## Hard Stops

Stop if the reset requires deleting working workflows, changing storage or packet contracts, adding routes/renderers, altering live/data/provider boundaries, replacing the official logo, publishing Founder B material, or combining multiple route redesigns into one sprint.
