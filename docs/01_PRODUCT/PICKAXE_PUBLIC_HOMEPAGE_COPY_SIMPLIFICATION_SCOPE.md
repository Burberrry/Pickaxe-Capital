# Pickaxe Public Homepage Copy Simplification Scope

Status: Docs-only copy and structure scope.
Date: 2026-06-21.
Authority: CEO B.
Runtime authority: None.

## 1. Current Problem

Pickaxe Capital has a working static/manual research system, strong source and risk doctrine, and a broad internal operating environment. That depth is useful after a user understands the product, but it creates too much internal operating-system complexity for a first-time public visitor.

The current public-facing experience asks the homepage to act as both the front door and the Alerts Desk. Internal modules, route language, research states, build concepts, and AI Habitat OS terminology appear before a visitor has a simple answer to what Pickaxe Capital is and why it exists.

The product is powerful, but the homepage copy needs to become simpler, sharper, and easier to read in public. The future front door should establish trust and purpose first, then offer a controlled path into the deeper research OS.

## 2. Homepage Goal

The future homepage should be a simple Pickaxe Capital front door that introduces the company before users enter the deeper AI Habitat OS.

In one short public journey, it should explain:

- what Pickaxe Capital is;
- who it is for;
- what problem it solves;
- why it exists;
- what a user can see or request;
- what it does not do;
- how it connects to AI Habitat OS;
- why source verification, risk review, and CEO B review matter.

The homepage should sell clarity, trust, infrastructure, discipline, and verified proof of work. It should not sell hype, prediction, trade execution, or guaranteed outcomes.

This document defines future copy and structure only. It does not authorize a homepage implementation, route change, renderer change, navigation change, or public release.

## 3. Public / Internal Split

### Public Front Door — Pickaxe Capital

Pickaxe Capital is the public company and product brand. The public front door explains the research-only market intelligence system in plain language and gives visitors a concise path to proof of work, system status, private access, or the internal research environment.

### Internal Operating System — AI Habitat OS

AI Habitat OS is the internal intelligence and operating-system layer inside Pickaxe Capital. It connects research intake, source verification, market analysis, risk checks, Alerts, Archive, Learning Ledger, Agent Habitat, and manual review. It should be introduced only after the visitor understands Pickaxe Capital.

### Command Identity — CEO B

CEO B is the symbolic masked command identity and the final review and decision layer. CEO B review is a human control gate, not an autonomous-agent claim and not a substitute for source or risk checks.

### Human Founder Layer — Founder B

Founder B is the separately governed human founder layer. Founder materials remain private and approval-required unless CEO B explicitly authorizes cleaned public use. Founder B must not be collapsed into, replaced by, or represented as the symbolic CEO B identity.

## 4. Recommended Homepage Sections

### A. Hero

Purpose: explain Pickaxe Capital in one clear sentence and establish the research-only, source-first, founder-led trust model.

Recommended content:

- one concise headline;
- one short explanatory subheadline;
- four trust badges;
- one primary call to action;
- one secondary proof-of-work action;
- restrained official branding and the existing Pickaxe visual language.

The first screen should not lead with internal route names, a module inventory, build tracking, speculative capabilities, or dense AI Habitat OS language.

### B. Problem

Purpose: show why Pickaxe Capital exists.

Traders, founders, and operators waste time across fragmented charts, filings, news, options flow, watchlists, social sentiment, screenshots, bookmarks, and market noise. Important evidence is difficult to compare, source quality is inconsistent, and context is often lost between tools.

The section should describe this fragmentation without implying that Pickaxe Capital currently has live provider connections or complete market coverage.

### C. Solution

Purpose: explain the system's method.

Pickaxe Capital organizes market research into structured packets that can move through source verification, analysis, risk review, and CEO B manual review. The value is disciplined evidence and decision infrastructure, not a stream of trade commands.

The section should make clear that the current system is private, static/manual, and research-only.

### D. Operating Loop

Purpose: make the Pickaxe method understandable at a glance.

Recommended loop:

```text
Capture -> Verify -> Analyze -> Risk Check -> CEO B Review -> Archive -> Learn
```

Each stage should remain human-governed:

- **Capture:** collect a research question, source, chart, filing, candidate, or observation.
- **Verify:** identify provenance, freshness, evidence quality, and unresolved conflicts.
- **Analyze:** organize the thesis, counter-thesis, scenario, and missing evidence.
- **Risk Check:** surface hard blocks, uncertainty, event risk, and no-trade conditions.
- **CEO B Review:** require the final human decision before anything moves forward.
- **Archive:** preserve the reviewed packet and its evidence lineage.
- **Learn:** propose lessons or improvements without autonomously changing rules.

### E. What Pickaxe Does

Purpose: explain the core capabilities without presenting future concepts as connected services.

- **Options Alert Candidates:** structures research-only candidates for source, contract-quality, risk, and manual review. Options involve substantial risk.
- **Source Verification:** records source quality, evidence state, and unresolved verification needs.
- **Research Packets:** turns fragmented observations into organized, reviewable research.
- **Market Command Center:** gives CEO B a structured view of research status, risk, and next manual actions.
- **AI Habitat OS:** connects the internal research, verification, risk, memory, and review environment.
- **Archive / Learning Ledger:** preserves packet lineage and unverified lesson candidates for later human review.
- **Visual Intelligence:** turns approved research context into clear internal visual artifacts without unsupported live-data claims.
- **Manual CEO B Review:** keeps a human decision gate above scores, agents, and workflow state.

Any later homepage implementation must distinguish implemented static/manual capabilities from future concepts. It must not imply live agents, active telemetry, provider connections, execution, publication, or automated learning.

### F. What Pickaxe Does Not Do

Purpose: provide direct compliance clarity.

Pickaxe Capital does not provide:

- financial advice;
- brokerage services;
- exchange services;
- trade execution;
- asset custody;
- guaranteed performance;
- autonomous trading;
- copy-trading;
- personalized buy or sell commands;
- an “AI trading bot” or signal-selling service.

### G. Proof of Work

Purpose: show verified internal build milestones rather than customer claims, performance claims, or invented scale.

Recommended proof categories:

- source-first Research Packet workflows;
- risk-gated Options Alert candidate review;
- Alerts Desk and CEO B review controls;
- Research Desk, Finance Terminal, and Visual Intelligence static/manual prototypes;
- Archive and Learning Ledger lineage;
- route, mirror, deployment, and safety validation;
- documented operating doctrine and locked product boundaries.

Proof must be tied to verifiable repository, validation, deployment, or product-state evidence. Do not use testimonials, assets-under-management claims, returns, win rates, accuracy, profit, user counts, institutional-client claims, or public-company claims unless independently verified and separately approved.

### H. Private Access / Founder-Led Build

Purpose: explain the present stage honestly.

Pickaxe Capital is being built as a private, founder-led research OS first. The system is designed to improve research discipline, preserve evidence, expose risk, and strengthen manual decision quality before any broader access model is considered.

Private access language must not imply an active subscription, entitlement system, customer account, waitlist backend, or guaranteed invitation. A future request action requires a separately approved collection and privacy workflow.

### I. Final Call to Action

Purpose: offer a small number of understandable next actions.

Recommended labels:

- **Enter Research OS**
- **View Founder Proof of Work**
- **Review System Status**
- **Request Private Access**

Recommended hierarchy:

1. Primary: **Enter Research OS**
2. Secondary: **View Founder Proof of Work**
3. Supporting text links: **Review System Status** and **Request Private Access**

These are copy and structure recommendations only. This sprint does not create links, forms, routes, navigation items, access controls, or request workflows. Every CTA destination and route mapping requires separate implementation approval.

## 5. Copy Principles

Future public homepage copy must:

- use fewer words;
- use stronger, plain-language headings;
- explain context before introducing jargon;
- lead with `Pickaxe Capital`;
- name `AI Habitat OS` only when explaining the internal operating layer;
- state current static/manual/private product truth;
- use institutional confidence without unsupported authority claims;
- explain the company as serious research infrastructure, not a demo;
- describe evidence quality and packet completeness, not expected return;
- keep source verification, risk review, and CEO B review visible;
- keep options-risk language visible wherever options capabilities appear;
- distinguish verified proof of work from future plans;
- use one clear primary action per section.

Future public homepage copy must not:

- use hype;
- claim fake scale;
- promise performance, profits, accuracy, or outcomes;
- claim live data, active providers, or real-time coverage without same-workflow verification;
- use “AI trading bot” framing;
- promise “signals that make money”;
- use buy, sell, enter, exit, copy, or execute instructions;
- imply autonomous agents, autonomous publishing, or autonomous rule changes;
- describe confidence or conviction as expected return;
- present a demo/static/manual value as live market truth;
- present Founder B material without approval;
- use speculative public-company ambition as current proof.
