- local receipt time may be used only for diagnostics;
- quote timestamp and trade timestamp are separate;
- underlying quote timestamp and options quote timestamp are separate;
- open interest cadence must be labeled or unavailable;
- IV and Greeks may be absent and must never be estimated silently;
- realtime and delayed data modes must be based on entitlement and response evidence;
- stale, missing, future, ambiguous, or unparseable timestamps block promotion;
- no public claim that data is live is allowed unless the provider response proves timestamp and data mode.

All user-facing values in any later public workflow would still require source, quote type, timestamp, timezone, and verification status from the same workflow.
