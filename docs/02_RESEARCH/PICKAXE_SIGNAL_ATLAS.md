# Pickaxe Signal Atlas

Status: Research taxonomy. No alert implementation approved.

## Purpose

Signal Atlas is a source-aware library of alternative indicators, unusual market tells, macro clues, consumer stress signals, supply chain signals, social sentiment signals, volatility signals, and disaster signals.

## Evidence Card

Every Atlas entry should document:

- canonical name
- signal family
- original source or methodology
- what it may proxy
- update cadence
- known limitations
- false-positive risks
- geographic scope
- source confidence
- independence group
- public/private classification
- last review

## Combination Rule

No single unusual indicator creates an alert.

Three or more independent signals may create a `Research Packet Candidate` only when:

- they come from genuinely independent evidence groups
- Source Hub verifies their provenance
- their time windows are compatible
- limitations and conflicts are visible
- Risk Desk hard blocks pass
- CEO B performs final review

## Market Cipher Room Relationship

Signal Atlas supplies documented clues. Market Cipher Room connects clues into a transparent investigation trail. Neither system may present coincidence, internet folklore, or speculation as verified fact.

## Safety

- no conspiracy claims
- no fake secret knowledge
- no unsupported historical claims
- no single-signal directional instruction
- no fake live monitoring
