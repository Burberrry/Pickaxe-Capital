# Pickaxe Quant Lab

Status: Future / Planned. Documentation only.

## Purpose

Quant Lab is the research academy for measurable rules, backtests, seasonality, scenarios, model evaluation, and disciplined rejection. It does not authorize live execution.

## Algorithmic Strategy Factory

1. Idea Intake
2. Source Hub Research
3. Signal Rule Sheet
4. Minimum Viable Python Spec
5. Backtest Configuration
6. Backtest Report
7. Risk Desk Review
8. Execution Readiness / Paper-Trade Boundary

## Required Strategy Card

- strategy name
- hypothesis
- source basis
- measurable variables
- universe
- timeframe
- entry and exit research rules
- transaction-cost assumption
- slippage assumption
- liquidity requirement
- parameter count
- in-sample period
- out-of-sample period
- benchmark
- drawdown limit
- failure criteria
- CEO B decision
- Archive outcome

## Hard Blocks

- no source
- no measurable variable
- look-ahead bias
- survivorship bias
- no transaction costs
- no slippage model
- too many parameters
- failed out-of-sample test
- unacceptable drawdown
- liquidity too low
- no CEO B review

## Output States

- Research Only
- Needs More Evidence
- Paper-Trade Candidate
- Manual Checklist Candidate
- Rejected
- Archived Lesson

## Market Cipher Room Relationship

Quant Lab may host the Market Cipher Room when a clue becomes a measurable hypothesis. Source Hub remains responsible for provenance and classification. The Cipher Room cannot turn speculation into fact or bypass hard blocks.
