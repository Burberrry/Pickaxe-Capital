# CEO B / Founder Identity System Scope

Status: Docs-only institutional scope.
Date: 2026-06-20.
Authority: CEO B.
Runtime authority: None.
Canonical visual lock: `docs/00_MASTER/CEO_B_VISUAL_IDENTITY_LOCK.md`.

This document defines the future identity system for CEO B and Founder B. It does not authorize image generation, public founder-photo use, runtime work, route changes, UI changes, or asset replacement.

## 1. Executive Identity Definition

- **CEO B** is the symbolic masked command identity for Pickaxe Capital and AI Habitat OS.
- **Founder B** is the human founder layer: the real builder, owner, reviewer, and public-company principal.
- Pickaxe Capital may use either layer only when the context, disclosure, and approval state are correct.
- AI Habitat OS should primarily use the masked CEO B identity because it represents command, governance, source discipline, risk control, and final human review.
- Human founder photographs, likeness-based portraits, biography materials, investor portraits, and public-company materials require separate CEO B approval before use.
- The two layers may support the same founder story, but they must never be visually or factually presented as the same kind of artifact.

Identity model:

```text
CEO B = symbolic masked command identity
Founder B = human founder identity
Pickaxe Capital = context-governed public brand layer
AI Habitat OS = masked command identity by default
```

## 2. Invariant CEO B Identity

Every CEO B visual must preserve:

- a full-face metallic-gold or antique-gold mask;
- black, charcoal, or gunmetal tailoring;
- controlled, restrained gold detailing;
- a composed executive posture;
- a dark institutional office, city, research, or command atmosphere;
- calm authority;
- no visible natural face;
- no aggression-first styling;
- no spectacle-first styling.

The identity must remain recognizable through the mask geometry, dark tailored silhouette, controlled metal finish, and composed posture. Scene, outfit, crop, lighting, and role details may change only within this invariant.

- **Used for:** governance, boardroom, policy, CEO B Profile, formal leadership, and restrained public-brand contexts.

- **Banned drift:** tuxedo spectacle, celebrity-red-carpet styling, oversized jewelry, crown symbolism, flashy luxury signaling.

No mask variants are authorized for generation by this scope. Future variants must use:

| Version | Working role | Required continuity |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `v2` | Refined institutional mask | Cleaner executive geometry; unmistakably derived from v1 |
| `v3` | Ceremonial mask | Elevated finish and restrained signature detail; no crown or costume drift |
| `v4` | Tactical risk-gate mask | Lower-gloss, risk-state finish; no militarization or aggression |
| `v5` | Public-company investor mask | Most restrained, formal, credible, and media-safe version |

Naming rules:

```text
ceo-b-mask-v{number}-{role}-{view}-{revision}.{ext}
ceo-b-{outfit-family}-mask-v{number}-{aspect}-{revision}.{ext}
```

- Use lowercase kebab-case.
- Include mask version, role or outfit, crop/aspect when relevant, and revision.
- Never use `final`, `final-final`, or ambiguous unversioned names.
- A new mask version requires side-by-side review against v1.
- A variant may refine the identity but may not create a different character.
- Canonical promotion requires explicit CEO B approval and a recorded replacement decision.

## 6. Public vs Private Use

### Public Site Acceptable Uses

- The approved canonical masked CEO B anchor.
- Approved versioned masked variants in context-appropriate placements.
- Human founder materials only after separate approval of the exact asset, crop, copy, and page.
- Clear brand or editorial treatment that does not imply documentary truth.

### Internal OS Acceptable Uses

- Masked CEO B identity for command, review, governance, source, risk, Archive, and milestone contexts.
- Low-frequency avatars, module headers, or approved status illustrations.
- Draft identity tests that remain local and are clearly labeled as drafts.

### Forbidden Public Uses

- Raw founder source photographs.
- Unapproved likeness-based founder imagery.
- Raw local paths, filenames containing private information, EXIF or other private metadata, or source-reference contact sheets.
- Face swaps or generated images described as documentary founder photographs.
- Images implying a real event, investor meeting, public listing, award, endorsement, press appearance, or track record that did not occur.

### Disclosure Boundary

- Generated masked CEO B imagery is symbolic brand artwork.
- A generated human-founder placeholder must be labeled internally as approval-required and must not be published.
- Public copy must not call generated identity art a candid, documentary, press, or historical photograph.
- Product-layer alt text should describe the visual without claiming a real photographed event.

## 8. Composition Rules

- Prefer a `4:5` master portrait.
- Preserve wide-hero crop options with safe space on at least one side for verified product-layer typography.
- Profile crops should retain the full mask, shoulders, and enough tailoring to identify the outfit family.
- Mobile-safe crops must keep both eyes and the mask center visible without relying on hidden overflow.
- Use one principal subject only.
- Do not generate readable text, financial numbers, dashboards, press logos, or badges inside the artwork.
- Reject distorted hands, duplicate fingers, malformed mask edges, asymmetrical eye openings, or broken tailoring.
- Do not generate fake company logos. Controlled Pickaxe marks should be added later in the verified product layer.
- The mask must remain visible and must not be hidden by props, extreme shadow, eyewear, hands, or crop.
- Avoid weapons, crowns, capes, armor, thrones, podium spectacle, cheering crowds, and fake ceremonial institutions.
- Backgrounds should support the role while remaining subordinate to the subject.
