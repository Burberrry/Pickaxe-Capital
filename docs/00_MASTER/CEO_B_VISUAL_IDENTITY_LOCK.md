## Core Identity

CEO B is represented inside Pickaxe Capital by one consistent symbolic identity:

- sculptural full-face metallic gold mask;
- black, charcoal, and gunmetal luxury tailoring;
- premium, controlled, institutional presence;
- restrained antique-gold Pickaxe Capital details;
- dark, powerful, founder-command energy;
- calm authority rather than aggression or spectacle.

The mask is the invariant. Clothing, environment, pose, and role-specific details may change.

```text
CEO B = same core masked identity + scene-appropriate outfit.
```

Every future CEO B visual must remain recognizable without relying on visible facial identity. The permanent recognition system is the gold mask, dark tailored silhouette, controlled gold detailing, and composed executive posture.

6. Ceremonial Pickaxe signature outfit — rare milestone, launch, annual-review, or brand-story use.

Outfit changes must never replace or obscure the core mask identity.

## Mask Rules

- Full-face metallic gold or antique-gold construction.
- Original Pickaxe-specific sculptural geometry.
- Dark eye openings; no exposed natural face.
- Premium brushed, polished, or lightly weathered metal depending on scene.
- No horror, superhero, cyberpunk, gladiator, or costume framing.
- No crown, weapon, military insignia, or excessive jewelry.
- Pickaxe marks remain subtle and controlled.
- Future mask variants must read as versions of one identity, not different characters.

## Founder Separation And Privacy

CEO B and the human founder are related but distinct visual layers:

- CEO B is the symbolic masked command identity used across AI Habitat OS and Pickaxe Capital operating visuals.
- Founder B is the real human founder used only in explicitly approved founder, biography, investor, or public-company materials.
- The supplied founder photograph remains a private source reference unless CEO B separately approves a cleaned public use.
- Do not publish raw local photo paths, personal metadata, or unapproved founder images.
- Do not use face-swapping to imply that an unverified generated portrait is a documentary photograph.
