# Pickaxe Design System

Status: Strategic design direction. No runtime changes approved.

## Design Thesis

Pickaxe is a premium dark founder command system: graphite, sovereign black, disciplined gold, restrained status color, precise typography, and calm information density.

Canonical visual phrase: **Pickaxe Sovereign Command System**.

## Originality Rule

Pickaxe may study broad qualities from admired references:

- the ambition and dramatic finance atmosphere associated with *Billions*
- the information density of Finviz
- the thematic research organization associated with ARK Invest
- the ticker-research anatomy of Fidelity
- the clue-trail feeling of mystery and code-breaking stories
- the premium command-center fantasy associated with Tony Stark

Pickaxe must not copy names, characters, plots, report text, logos, layouts, interface trade dress, imagery, scenes, sounds, props, or protected brand assets. Every feature must be translated into original Pickaxe language and workflow.

## Visual Language

- sovereign black and executive graphite foundations
- champagne or antique gold for priority and ownership
- ivory for readable primary text
- sage for verified states
- brick or amber for risk and blocked states
- subtle grids, lineage lines, and evidence trails
- crisp cards with restrained depth
- motion limited to transforms and opacity

## Product-Specific Language

### Market Cipher Room

- archive-room atmosphere
- clue trail
- cipher wheel
- source lineage
- hidden-pattern geometry
- gold-on-graphite surfaces

Avoid religious-conspiracy imagery, copyrighted film/book scenes, and fake secret-knowledge theatrics.

### Market Overview

- one-screen command sheet
- compact scan hierarchy
- heatmaps and breadth with explicit timestamps
- no imitation of Finviz branding or exact layout

### Pickaxe Research Center

- report library
- theme shelves
- source confidence
- research status
- original report templates

### Security Research Cockpit

- clean ticker identity
- structured research anatomy
- strong source trail
- no transactional trade controls

### Founder OS

- private, calmer, and more human than Market Habitat
- no health data on public surfaces
- no blending hobbies or lifestyle data into Options Alerts

## Motion

Motion should suggest system state, review flow, and source lineage. It must never imply unbuilt live monitoring or autonomous activity.

## Copy Voice

- direct
- calm
- strategic
- evidence-first
- risk-aware
- ambitious without hype
- explicit about current versus future capability
