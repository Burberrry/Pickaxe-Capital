# UI Polish Backlog

Purpose: non-feature polish backlog for Pickaxe Capital / AI Habitat OS. This file should guide future visual cleanup without starting a new product sprint.

## Guardrails

- Do not change product scope while polishing.
- Do not start Options Hub.
- Do not rebuild Agents.
- Do not add live APIs, provider adapters, broker execution, betting/sportsbook integrations, copy-trading, or fake live data.
- Do not expose Obsidian notes.
- Keep Watchlists stable unless a visible bug is found.
- Preserve the graphite/gold private market OS shell.

## Alerts And Signals Polish

- Strengthen source-required and manual-review labels.
- Avoid fake urgency.
- Avoid "prediction", "guaranteed", "buy", "sell", "executed", or "real-time".
- Make Alerts feel like research review packets, not trade triggers.
- Make Signals feel like research context, not calls.

## Mobile And Responsive Checks

- Verify no horizontal overflow on `/`, `#/dashboard`, `#/watchlists`, `#/source-hub`, `#/signals`, `#/alerts`, `#/agents`, and `#/staging`.
- Watch for right-side panels, long labels, fixed columns, and card grids.
- Prefer `minmax(0, 1fr)`, `min-width: 0`, `max-width: 100%`, and earlier stacking over global overflow hiding.

## Sidebar And Navigation Checks

- Keep number-only route badges from `00` through `20`.
- Keep graphite/gold active states.
- Avoid ticker-style abbreviations in route badges.
- Reduce subtitle clutter only if it improves scanability.
- Do not rename routes without updating planning docs and handoff context.

## Animation Restraint Rules

- Use motion only for subtle state and system atmosphere.
- Prefer opacity and transform animations.
- Avoid busy pulsing, fake telemetry, or anything that implies real live background work.
- No rainbow/casino/crypto chaos.
- No cartoon AI energy.

## Copy Polish Rules

- Use "static", "manual", "demo", "source-required", "local-only", and "CEO B review required" where appropriate.
- Avoid "live", "real-time", "connected", "autonomous", "executed", "prediction", "guaranteed", "buy", "sell", and "profit" unless used in a negative safety boundary.
- If an external source title contains risky words, frame it as an external source title, not an app capability.
