# Pickaxe Capital

## Verified market research. Risk-gated conviction. CEO B reviewed.

Pickaxe Capital is a premium AI-powered research operating system that turns market noise into source-backed, risk-aware intelligence.

It does not sell predictions. It does not execute trades. It does not guarantee outcomes. It does not act as a broker, investment adviser, exchange, or trade execution service.

Pickaxe Capital sells the pickaxe: the research tools, verification workflow, risk discipline, agent review, and CEO B human judgment that help users build conviction before they act.

## The Problem

Markets are overloaded with noise: fake certainty, bad screenshots, recycled headlines, unverified chart levels, low-quality options flow, and hype disguised as research.

Most tools try to produce more output.

Pickaxe Capital is different.

It decides whether output deserves to exist.

## The System

Every market idea must pass the Pickaxe ritual:

`Market Input → Source Ledger → Agent Habitat → Silence Gate → Risk Gate → CEO B Review → Public Research Card → Memory Vault`

## Why Source Ledger Matters

Source Ledger prevents fake data from entering public output. Every price, chart level, headline, options chain, screenshot, and market number must be verified, timestamped, and labeled before it can be used.

## Why Silence Gate Matters

No Output is a valid result. Pickaxe Capital should not generate research cards just because something moved. If the evidence is weak, sources conflict, risk/reward is poor, or the thesis is unclear, the system stays silent.

The system earns trust not only by what it says, but by what it refuses to say.

## Why Risk Gate Matters

Risk Gate blocks incomplete or dangerous research cards.

No invalidation means no approval.
Unverified price means no approval.
Illiquid options mean no approval.
No CEO B Review means no approval.

## Why CEO B Review Matters

CEO B is the final human authority.

AI agents prepare research. CEO B approves, rejects, edits, archives, escalates, or chooses No Output.

This keeps Pickaxe Capital human-reviewed, brand-protected, and disciplined.
